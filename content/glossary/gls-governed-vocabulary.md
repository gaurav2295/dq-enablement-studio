## Definition

The rule that key issues, their names and their value levers are **created or changed only by
an explicit owner ruling** — never invented by an agent or a consultant mid-engagement.

## Usage

The reason is comparability: the moment two engagements name the same problem differently, nothing
aggregates and the value library stops being a library.

Agents and consultants may **propose**, and may apply *owner-ratified principles* to a bulk queue —
recording the principle, the method and the date on every row ([[gls-bulk-ratification]]). What
they may not do is coin a term autonomously.
