## Definition

The date the underlying data was extracted — one of the three things that must be visible wherever
a currency figure appears, alongside the [[gls-value-lever|value lever]] and the
[[gls-confidence-label|confidence label]].

## Usage

Data ages. A defect population measured in March and quoted in September is not wrong, but it is
**as of March**, and saying so is what keeps the number defensible when someone re-runs it.

The rule is simple and absolute: no `$` figure without a lever, an as-of date and a confidence
label ([[prn-value-discipline]]).
