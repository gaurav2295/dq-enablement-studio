## Definition

One of the five attributes a key issue carries: **what this data problem threatens in a
transformation** — a load that fails, a process that cannot run, a control that cannot be
evidenced.

## Usage

Readiness risk is the transformation-facing half of a key issue, where
[[gls-value-lever|value lever]] is the money-facing half. It is what makes a key issue legible to a
programme manager who does not care about data quality as a topic but cares a great deal about a
go-live date.

State it as a consequence, not a category: "duplicate vendors block automated payment matching at
cutover" says more than "high risk".
