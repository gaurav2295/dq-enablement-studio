## Definition

The **results artefact** an executed rule set produces and [[gls-vector-studio|vector-studio]]
consumes — rule by rule, the counts that were measured in the client's environment.

## Usage

The evaluation sheet is the boundary that keeps the tooling out of client systems: rules are
executed on the engagement's own environment and only the **result sheet** travels. Nothing in the
blueprint chain ingests customer data.

It carries [[gls-evidenced-volume|evidenced volumes]], not currency — valuation happens afterwards
at the lever, from documented assumptions.
