## Definition

**How the engagement's work divides** (A24) — and the banked concept behind both capacity
validation and the solution explanation.

| Lane | Who | Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Qualitative** | Lead + Delivery Partner (C5/C6) | Engaging key stakeholders on the business outcomes; highlighting complexities from the questionnaires |
| **Quantitative** | GDC | Executing the rules in scope; extracting the tables those rules need |

## Usage

The lanes do double duty, which is why they are worth naming once and reusing:

- **Internally** they carry hours and a live capacity check. The standing rule: *do not quote a
  duration the capacity check refuses.* See [[gls-time-to-value]].
- **Client-facing** they carry **no hours at all** — just who does what — rendered in
  [[gls-solution-proposal]] and the detailed document.

> [!important]
> One concept, two renderings. Do not create a separate client-facing "team model" alongside the
> lanes; the client view is the same lanes with the hours removed.

That second use — the **solution explanation** — is what makes the split worth showing at all:
the qualitative lane is work only the client's own stakeholders can supply, and no amount of
quantitative execution substitutes for it. It is the answer when a client asks why their people
need to be in the room.
