## Definition

The arithmetic a [[gls-value-lever|value lever]] uses to turn an
[[gls-evidenced-volume|evidenced volume]] into money — volume × a documented assumption, owned
separately from the SQL that produced the volume.

## Usage

> [!important]
> **Volumes ≠ value.** SQL certifies counts and populations; no SQL the methodology emits ever
> produces a currency figure. The formula is where the count becomes a number with a currency sign,
> and it is deliberately a separate, visible, editable artefact.

Every figure it produces is [[gls-indicative|indicative]] and carries its
[[gls-as-of-date|as-of date]] and [[gls-confidence-label|confidence label]]. Change an assumption
and the number changes in front of the client — that is the feature, not a flaw.
