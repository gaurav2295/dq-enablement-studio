## Definition

The **count** a DQ rule certifies in the client's own data — how many records fail, out of how many
— and the link in the chain between a rule and a currency figure.

## Usage

The spine reads: business outcome → value lever → DQ rule → **evidenced volume** → quantified
value → prioritised action. Evidenced volume is the last honest, machine-produced number in that
sequence; everything after it involves an assumption someone owns.

It is a defect population, not a valuation, and it is quoted with its denominator — see
[[gls-opportunity-count]].
