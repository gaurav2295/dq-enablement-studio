## Definition

**The client-facing framing of engagement duration**, at home in the Solution Proposal mode.

## Usage

Duration is a single-owner concept in the instrument:

| Where | What it carries |
|---|---|
| Canvas | **No duration at all** |
| Solution Proposal (mode) | Time to value — the client-facing framing, no commercials |
| Engagement / the detailed document | The **priced** timeline |

Keeping duration out of the canvas is deliberate: the canvas answers *what is worth doing*, and
a duration on that screen pulls the conversation into delivery before the outcome is agreed.

> [!warning]
> A stated time to value must survive the [[gls-work-lanes]] capacity check. **Do not quote a
> duration the capacity check refuses** — the check runs internally even though the client-facing
> framing shows no hours.
