## Definition

Two three-letter names, one letter apart, meaning different things:

| Acronym | Expands to | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **BOB** | Business Outcomes **Blueprint** | The **offering** |
| **BOA** | Business Outcomes **Activation** | The **engine repo** |

## Usage

Write the acronym out on first use in any document that mentions both. The distinction matters
in practice because BOA supplies things BOB consumes — the value library that defines the 18
[[gls-value-lever]] entries and the six [[gls-outcome-category]] buckets live in BOA.

> [!warning]
> The collision is **logged as D10 and unresolved**. Do not silently rename either side to fix
> it; renaming is an owner decision, and both names are already referenced across files and
> registers.

The DQ variant of the offering is [[gls-bob-dq]].
