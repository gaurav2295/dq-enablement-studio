## Definition

The finished artefact the offering is named for: a **valued, prioritised, sequenced** account of
what the client's data is costing them and what to do about it — outcomes at the top, evidence
underneath, actions in order.

## Usage

The blueprint is the *up* direction of the chain. The canvas walks **down** from an outcome to the
rules that measure it to sell the work; the blueprint walks **up** from executed rules to
quantified value to prove it. Same spine, two directions.

It is assembled in [[gls-vector-studio|vector-studio]] from the
[[gls-evaluation-sheet|evaluation sheet]] — vector-studio never authors SQL, and the blueprint
never invents a figure that the evidence does not carry.
