## Definition

An object that spans two [[gls-sow|Statements of Work]], carrying an explicit **primary** and
**secondary** owner rather than appearing twice.

## Usage

Crossover exists to stop the same object being cleansed twice, differently, by two teams. The
primary owner drives the work; the secondary consumes and confirms.

The primary/secondary assignment is a **governance decision** recorded in the programme's data
readiness charter, not something the plan infers — an unassigned crossover is an open item, and
should be visible as one.
