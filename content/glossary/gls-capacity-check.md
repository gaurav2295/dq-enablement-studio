## Definition

The internal test of whether the quoted duration is deliverable with the people actually
available — hours per [[gls-work-lanes|lane]] against the shape of the engagement.

## Usage

> [!important]
> **Do not quote a duration the capacity check refuses.** It is the one hard constraint on
> [[gls-time-to-value|time to value]]: the client-facing framing may be simple, but it may not be
> optimistic.

The check lives in the [[gls-internal-workbench|internal workbench]] and never appears in a client
instrument. Its inputs — the throughput ladder, object and complexity factors, SME allowance — are
in the resourcing rubric ([[prc-estimate-blueprint-effort]]).
