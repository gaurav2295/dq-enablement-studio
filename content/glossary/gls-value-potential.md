## Definition

**The indicative scale of the opportunity evidenced in the client's data.**

Explicitly **not**: booked value, a forecast, or a promised saving.

## Usage

Value potential is the headline number the instrument is allowed to show. It is bounded on both
sides — *evidenced in the client's data* rules out generic benchmarks, and *indicative* (see
[[gls-indicative]]) rules out presenting it as a commitment.

It is always a **defensible subset, not a gross total**. Exclusions stay excluded and stay
flagged; distinct lenses are never summed.

## Say / never say

- **Say**: "the value potential evidenced in your data, on these assumptions, is X".
- **Never say**: "you will save X", "we have identified X in savings", "X of benefit booked".

> [!warning]
> Volumes are not value. SQL certifies counts and populations; currency is volume multiplied by
> a documented assumption owned separately at the [[gls-value-lever]].
