## Definition

The **end-to-end business-process** grouping above process areas — a Source-to-Contract /
Procure-to-Pay / Plan-to-Maintain / Transportation & Logistics / Record-to-Report / Enabling &
Foundational style map. An object maps to one [[gls-process-area|process area]] and, through it, to
one BPA.

## Usage

BPA is the **strategic** lens: outcome tiles, KPI tracking and programme roll-ups report by BPA,
because BPA is meant to mirror how the business thinks about its own processes rather than how the
cleanse team organises its work.

Treat the six names as **provisional** on any engagement — confirm them against the client's own
end-to-end process taxonomy rather than importing the list verbatim.
