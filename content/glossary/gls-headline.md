## Definition

The single figure the instrument shows for what is currently selected — the top line of
[[gls-the-package|the package]].

## Usage

Two rules keep it defensible:

- **It aggregates at the lever and only at the lever.** Summing key issues or rules into a headline
  double-counts, because several rules evidence one lever ([[prn-value-discipline]]).
- **Only a selection moves it.** Exploring a rule proposes a [[gls-band|band]]; it does not change
  the headline until someone promotes or adds it.

The headline is [[gls-value-potential|value potential]], never booked value or a promised saving.
