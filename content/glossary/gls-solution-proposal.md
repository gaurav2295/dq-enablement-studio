## Definition

**One client-facing name at two depths.**

| Depth | What it is | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| The **mode** | The journey, the three phases, time-to-value | No commercials |
| The **document** ("Solution Proposal — Detailed") | The same content plus the timeline and the indicative-investment annex | Commercials; generated in Engagement |

## Usage

Both depths **render from the same `PHASES` / journey source, so they cannot drift**. That
single-source rule is the whole reason one name can safely cover two artefacts: the deeper one
adds material, it never restates the shallower one differently.

The mode belongs to [[gls-client-instrument]]; the document is generated in
[[gls-internal-workbench]] and is that tier's one client-visible output.

Duration appears here as [[gls-time-to-value]] in the mode, and as a priced timeline in the
document. The canvas carries no duration at all.

> [!warning]
> If you find yourself editing the phase text in one depth only, stop — the shared source has
> been bypassed and the two will drift, which is the exact failure this design prevents.
