## Definition

**The accumulating answer-so-far**: what is selected, what it's worth, what evidences it.

Visible from every mode. **Owns no question.** Never shows cost, duration or delivery shape.

> [!warning]
> **Two things are called "the spine".** bob-dq's `CONTEXT.md` gives the package that nickname;
> elsewhere in bob-dq — and throughout this knowledge base — *the spine* means the chain itself,
> `business outcome → value lever → key issue → DQ rule → evidenced volume → quantified value`,
> and its organisational reading the L1–L5 hierarchy. See [[con-value-chain]] and
> [[con-outcome-hierarchy-l1-l5]]. Prefer **"the package"** for this unit's referent; if you
> write *spine*, say which one you mean.

## Usage

The package is the continuity device of the instrument — the reason a session can move between
modes without the room losing the thread. Each mode answers exactly one question and refuses the
others; the package is the exception that proves the rule, because it answers nothing and merely
carries the running state.

Its three columns map onto the chain: **selected** (outcomes and levers), **worth**
([[gls-value-potential]], aggregated at the lever), **evidenced by** (the rules behind it).

Selection state is written by the canvas *Add* action and, equivalently, by *Promote* in the
rule explorer — see [[gls-propose-and-promote]].

> [!warning]
> Cost, duration and delivery shape are structurally absent, not merely hidden. If a
> cost-shaped field appears on the package, the tier boundary in
> [[gls-client-instrument]] has been breached.
