## Definition

One of the instrument's screens, each answering **exactly one question** and refusing the others:
Introduction (why are we here?), Canvas (which outcomes?), Value Studio (what is it worth?), Rule
Explorer (what evidences it?), Solution Proposal (how would this go?) — plus the internal
Engagement mode.

## Usage

Modes are a discipline, not navigation. If two modes answer the same question, one of them is wrong.
The five client-facing modes make up the
[[gls-client-instrument|client instrument]]; Engagement is the
[[gls-internal-workbench|internal workbench]].

[[gls-the-package|The package]] is visible from every mode and owns no question of its own — it is
the answer-so-far, not a sixth mode.
