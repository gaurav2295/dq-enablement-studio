## Definition

**The tier of modes that may face a client room** (A22, 2026-08-05):

- Introduction
- Canvas
- Value Studio
- Rule Explorer
- Solution Proposal

## Usage

The client tier is defined by what it **carries nothing of**: no investment, no rates, no hours,
no internal tool names, anywhere. That is a hard property of the tier, not a per-screen review
step — anything commercial or internal belongs in [[gls-internal-workbench]].

Every figure in the client instrument is [[gls-indicative]]; that is the tier's standing word.

> [!warning]
> "No internal tool names anywhere" includes the instrument names — [[gls-bob-canvas]],
> [[gls-dq-studio]], [[gls-vector-studio]], [[gls-rule-repository]], [[gls-ctx-server]]. They
> are internal vocabulary and stay out of the room.

The one internal-tier artefact that legitimately reaches a client is the finished
[[gls-solution-proposal]] document, generated in Engagement and presented full-screen.
