## Definition

**Computed from stated assumptions that the reader can open and change.**

*Indicative* is the **standing word for every figure in the client instrument**. Not a hedge —
a description of how the number was made and an invitation to inspect it.

## Usage

A figure earns the word by satisfying the transparency test: the assumptions behind it are
stated, reachable and editable by the reader. If the reader cannot open the assumption, the
figure is not indicative — it is asserted, and it does not belong in the instrument.

Every `$` figure appears with a visible [[gls-value-lever]], an **as-of date** and a
**confidence label**. That is how "always give the number" and "never inflate" coexist.

## Say / never say

- **Say**: "indicative".
- **Never say**: **"directional"**, **"projected"**. Both are banned: *directional* implies a
  trend the data does not support, and *projected* implies a forecast the method does not make.
- **Never say**: any return ratio. ROI is excluded from the offering's language everywhere
  (R4, 2026-08-04); investment and return enter only after real data is profiled.
