## Definition

One of the five attributes a key issue carries: **how badly it bites** — the prioritisation
signal that decides what gets worked first.

## Usage

Criticality is a judgement, and it is recorded as one. It is not derived from defect volume: a
small number of broken records on a critical data element can outrank a large number on a field
nobody uses ([[ref-sap-key-fields-and-critical-data-elements]]).

> [!warning]
> Criticality is **not** value. A key issue carries no currency — money aggregates at the lever and
> only at the lever ([[prn-value-discipline]]).
