## Definition

The contracted scope document a workstream is delivered under. On a multi-workstream cleanse
programme every object belongs to exactly one SoW — for example an Integrated Supply Chain (ISC)
workstream and an Enterprise Asset Management & Finance workstream.

## Usage

The SoW is a **structural** attribute in the cleanse model, not just paperwork: it decides who
owns an object, which plan it appears in, and which team's capacity it consumes.

Objects that genuinely span two SoWs are handled by an explicit crossover relationship rather
than by duplication.
