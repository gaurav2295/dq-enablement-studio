## Definition

The delivery team that executes the **quantitative** work lane — running the rules in scope and
extracting the tables those rules need.

## Usage

The two [[gls-work-lanes|work lanes]] split by *what the work is*, not by seniority: the
qualitative lane (Lead + Delivery Partner) engages stakeholders on outcomes and surfaces
complexity from the questionnaires; the GDC lane executes.

Internally the lanes carry hours and feed the [[gls-capacity-check|capacity check]]. Client-facing
they carry no hours at all — just who does what.
