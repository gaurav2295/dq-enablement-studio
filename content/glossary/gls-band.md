## Definition

The **range** a value is shown as rather than a single number — the honest presentation of a figure
computed from assumptions that could reasonably differ.

## Usage

A band is **greyed** while it is only *proposed* — stated, not claimed. A rule picked in the
explorer proposes the measures its lever reaches; only a facilitator **Promote**, or a canvas Add,
moves the selection into [[gls-headline|the headline]] ([[gls-propose-and-promote]]).

The greying is doing real work: it is what lets a room explore a number without the room hearing it
as a commitment.
